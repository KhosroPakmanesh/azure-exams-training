# Northstar Travel: Changes and cancellation policy (fictional example)

For standard itinerary reservations, customers who cancel at least **7 days** before departure are eligible for a full refund. Reservations canceled within 7 days of departure are not eligible for a full refund under the standard fare rules.

After an eligible cancellation is approved, refunds are typically processed within **5 business days**. Bank processing can add more time. Change requests are handled through the booking support team. Terms for promotional and nonrefundable fares may differ.

These examples are invented for training and are not policies of a real travel provider.
